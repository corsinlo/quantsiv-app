TLS domains

Quantsiv opens one TLS connection to a domain you own and records the protocol version, cipher suite, key-exchange group where the server reveals it, and the certificate's algorithm, key size and expiry. It never connects to a domain until you have proved ownership with a DNS TXT record, and it checks the record again before every scan. Only port 443 is scanned, and only public addresses are reached.
