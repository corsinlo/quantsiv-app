Why this matters now

Adversaries can record encrypted data today and decrypt it once a cryptographically relevant quantum computer exists. EO 14412 (22 June 2026) requires post-quantum key establishment on high-value assets and high-impact systems by 31 December 2030, and post-quantum digital signatures by 31 December 2031. It also directs CISA, in coordination with NIST, to publish guidance on minimum elements for a Cryptographic Bill of Materials (CBOM) within 270 days.

Quantsiv is being built to produce a CycloneDX 1.6 CBOM for every scan and to rank findings by how long the protected data must stay confidential. Neither is available yet; scores shown today are severity scores only.
